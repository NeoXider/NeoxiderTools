## Substantial improvement proposal

### Proposal A: bounded stroke processing with separate adapters (not implemented)

**Problem and evidence.** `Drawer` combines legacy input, polyline storage, smoothing, renderer/material ownership, physics, events, and pooling. `maxPoints` is applied only after smoothing; it neither bounds raw points nor intermediate arrays. The current Chaikin pass doubles point count: 1,000 raw points with 10 passes produce 1,024,000 intermediate points before the output is reduced. Live following now needs additional collider projection allocations. This is a reproducible structural growth estimate, not a measured profiler result. The mutable public lists also allow callers to bypass metric/resource invariants.

**Proposed architecture/API.** Introduce an ordinary C# `StrokeBuilder` with `StrokeOptions`, `TryAppend(Vector3)`, `Clear()`, `RawCount`, `Distance`, and `CopyProcessedPoints(List<Vector3> destination)`. Add opt-in `MaxRawPoints` / `MaxProcessedPoints`, a documented overflow policy (`RejectNewest`, `DropOldest`, or `Simplify`), endpoint-preserving reduction during smoothing, reusable working buffers, and snapshot-based completion. Separate `DrawerInputAdapter` from renderer/collider adapters; `Drawer` remains the convenience facade. Keep projection/input replaceable so projects can provide Input System, stylus, network, or replay points without a main camera or scene search. A dedicated view adapter owns materials and a renderer lease until disposal.

**Compatibility/migration.** Retain Drawer fields, UnityEvents, misspelled serialized `useFolow`, and all current methods. Map existing settings into the new model; default raw capacity remains unlimited and legacy `maxPoints <= 1` behavior stays unchanged. Offer bounded policies explicitly rather than silently changing old strokes. Preserve the legacy `rawPoints` list at the facade boundary initially, documenting that direct mutation bypasses invariants; expose read-only snapshots in the new API. Avoid mandatory scene or NoCode dependencies in the core.

**Acceptance scenarios.** The same scripted stroke produces equivalent endpoints/distance in the core and Drawer; 100,000 submitted points respect configured raw/processed capacities; 0/1/2 points and disabled limits retain documented semantics; caller buffers can be reused without allocating per accepted point after capacity is established; looping and variable-width collider policies are explicit; replayed point streams work without input/camera objects; metric callbacks may finish a stroke without losing or mutating the next snapshot. Verify performance with profiler allocation captures rather than source-only claims.

**Effort/risk.** About 3-5 engineering days plus profiling and compatibility review. Main risks are public-list mutation compatibility, changing Chaikin output/order, and keeping event snapshots stable while wrappers remain serialized. Deliver the core and opt-in adapters first; migrate the facade only after differential geometry regressions pass.

### Proposal B: pool registration and material lease ownership (not implemented; cross-boundary)

**Problem.** Drawer now destroys its automatic source GameObject, but `PoolManager` retains the source-keyed pool, inactive clones, and `_prefab` closure until the manager dies. Its public API has no per-pool removal. Directly returning a Drawer renderer through PoolManager and acquiring it in another Drawer can also retain the first Drawer's material record; checking `SpawnGeneration` prevents stale timed return, but does not transfer native material ownership.

**Design/API.** Coordinate with `Neo.Tools.Spawner` to provide `RegisterPool(prefab)` returning a reference-counted registration lease, plus a generation-aware instance lease with release/dispose callbacks. Drawer's automatic source registration and all its clone materials would belong to these leases. The last registration release clears inactive objects and removes the source key; active instances are retired on return. Material restoration/cleanup runs on every pool return, including external return and capacity eviction. Avoid a blind `RemovePool` that destroys instances used by another consumer.

**Compatibility/migration.** Retain existing `Get`/`Release` overloads and assigned prefab behavior. Introduce opt-in registration/instance leases, then move Drawer internals to them without changing serialized/public Drawer members. Existing external returns remain supported by the Spawner API; the new release callback closes resource ownership explicitly.

**Acceptance/effort/risk.** Repeatedly create/destroy 100 automatic-source Drawers and verify pool/source/material counts return to baseline; share one assigned prefab between two Drawers and ensure disposing one does not retire the other's lines; return/reissue externally and verify neither old timers nor owner destruction affects the new material/lease. Estimate 2-3 days across both assembly owners; risks are pool eviction during outstanding leases, callback order, and scene teardown. This worker did not edit Spawner.
